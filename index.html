ATTEMPT 1

Sneaky Santa

Sneak through the house, leave the presents under the tree and get back up the chimney without waking the family.

  • Rooms stay dark until you open their door. Careful: opening a door wakes anyone asleep inside.
  • If someone looks your way, hide in a cupboard, under a table or in the shadows.
  • The cookies and the milk are hidden in different rooms. Find both for bonus points.

Touch: arrow buttons to walk · JUMP · ACTION (hide, stairs, deliver)
Keys: ←→ walk · ↑↓ stairs · Space jump · E action
